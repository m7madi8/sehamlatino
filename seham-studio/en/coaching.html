Coaching questions

Is coaching psychotherapy?

No. Coaching is not a substitute for psychological or medical treatment.

Are sessions confidential?

Privacy is central. During practical training, topics may be discussed with an academic supervisor in a professional confidential setting.

Are there fees?

During Seham’s practical training, a nominal fee is agreed directly with her.

Do I need a clear goal?

No. Sessions can start by exploring what feels unclear.
