Seham Atallah / My story

My story, through movement.

Dance was the first place where all these parts of me could come together — my own body.

A space to be myself

I grew up as an Arab girl in a community where dance was not always accepted as it was, especially when it was associated with femininity, the body, presence and allowing oneself to be seen.

I loved dancing, yet I had many questions. Was this kind of dance acceptable? How did others see me? Could I be a woman who dances, expresses herself through her body and loves her femininity without having to justify it?

Somewhere between my love of dance and all those voices around and within me, I began discovering something new. I was not just looking for a way to dance; I was looking for a space where I could be myself, Seham.

A space to be feminine without shame, strong without being hard on myself, sensitive without feeling weak, and bold without apologizing.

Dance became more than movement. It changed how I saw myself and life, taught me to listen to my body instead of judging it, and helped me discover my femininity, voice and parts of myself that did not have enough space in everyday life. Expressing ourselves does not mean abandoning our values or identity.

That was the beginning of my real relationship with dance: no longer just a passion or a profession, but a free space where I discovered myself.

Seham Atallah

About me

Seham Atallah

My journey was not clear from the beginning. I started dancing at five, practicing ballet, hip-hop, jazz and Zumba. I also explored drawing, sculpture and fashion design, seeing the body as a space for expressing beauty.

After years of exploring different dance forms, I arrived at Latin dance. Its energy, joy and expression of life felt like a language for a part of me that had long been searching for itself.

From dance to life coaching, I discovered the connection between what we experience in our bodies and what we carry within. My love of working with people also led me to group facilitation through psychodrama, where expression, consciousness, movement and the body–soul relationship meet.

These were not separate paths for me, but pieces of the same story:

  • how we see ourselves.
  • how we inhabit our bodies.
  • and how much space we allow ourselves in the world.

Each experience deepened my understanding of the relationship between body, soul and thought. Together they made me who I am today: a woman combining art, movement, design and awareness, helping others discover and express themselves more freely through Bachata Lady Style, movement and conversations with other women.

Dance has never been just steps for me. It is a relationship with the body, the soul and others.

Vision

A language without translation

Seham Latino is a global platform that brings together dance, confidence, awareness, expression, creativity, personal freedom and the connection between body and spirit.

I want to reach women from different cultures and backgrounds and create a shared language that does not need translation: movement.

Sometimes the body knows how to say things words cannot. Change does not always begin with an idea; sometimes it begins with a small movement, a posture, a breath, a look or a step.

I am building a world where a woman can be more than one version of herself:

strong and sensitive gentle and bold feminine and powerful playful and deep

She does not have to choose just one side to be accepted.

For every woman who enters my space, I want her to discover that within her are many things worth moving, being seen, and taking up space in her life.
