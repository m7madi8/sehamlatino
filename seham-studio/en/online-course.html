01   /   THE EXPERIENCE

About the course

A home course for women who want to learn bachata: not just memorizing steps, but feeling their bodies, moving confidently and finding their own style.

Ten recorded sessions combine technique, body awareness and expression. Each practice begins with a moment of reflection before turning an idea into movement.

From the basics to Lady Style, step by step and without prior experience. Build a strong foundation, tools for expression and greater awareness of your body.

Is this course for you?

For complete beginners and women returning to dance after a break. Learn at home, without a partner and without pressure to be ready from day one.

For women looking for more than steps: a connection with the body, confidence and an expression that feels personal.

02   /   THE METHOD

Your learning journey

Ten progressive recorded lessons, approximately 15–25 minutes each. You have a full year from enrollment to revisit any lesson and practice at your own pace.

Watch on any device; a computer or tablet can make movement details easier to see. If possible, use a mirror to observe your hands, hips and posture. Choose a small, safe, comfortable area and clothing you can move freely in. There is no special outfit required.

Do not rush through the course. The aim is to learn, practice and notice the difference in your confidence and movement.

  1. 01

    A strong foundation

    Basic steps, rhythm, posture, presence and comfort in your own body.

  2. 02

    Your body’s language

    Hand, hip, foot, shoulder and body techniques for natural, connected movement.

  3. 03

    Movement meets expression

    Waves, isolations and upper/lower body coordination.

  4. 04

    A complete dance and empowerment session

    Bring everything together, then explore confidence and self-expression.
